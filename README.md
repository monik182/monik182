## Selected projects

### 🎬 Cinefile

A personal movie library that turns saved Instagram recommendations into structured, searchable media.

It extracts recommendations from reels, captions, images and carousels, matches them with real movie data, and keeps everything organized in one place.

The app itself is private. A case study covering the product decisions, architecture and iterations is coming soon.

### 🗂️ [Sheet to Kanban](https://github.com/monik182/sheet-to-kanban)

A pixel-art Kanban interface built on top of Google Sheets.

The Sheet stays the source of truth while the app adds drag-and-drop workflows, filtering, real-time write-back and an integrated Claude workspace for developing product ideas.

### 🌙 After Dark Screening Generator

A small private tool I built for my cinema club.

I give it natural-language screening details — movie, cinema, date, ticket link and notes — and it consistently generates the bilingual event copy in the exact structure I need.

It also adapts the output based on the cinema and screening details.
